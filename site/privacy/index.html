Policy note · effective 27 August 2026

Privacy, in plain language

OpenAPI Collection Bridge is designed so your API definitions and collections stay on your device.

Conversion data

The CLI has no telemetry and makes no network requests. The browser specimen converts content in memory. It does not upload, retain, or log pasted files; reloading the page clears them.

License data

If you paste or return with a Pro license, the token and a time-stamped verification result are stored in your browser’s local storage under sb_license:openapi-collection-bridge. The token is sent only to the Sociobot billing verification endpoint, at most once per day, to confirm validity. You can clear it through your browser’s site-data controls.

Payments and counts

Purchases are handled by Sociobot/Dodo as merchant of record. Their checkout privacy terms apply to payment details. This site ships no advertising, analytics, fingerprinting, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the project’s public source repository. Do not include credentials or private API definitions.
